Privacy Policy — JAPAM COUNT

Effective date: 28 October 2026

JAPAM COUNT: Mantra Counter ("JAPAM COUNT", "the app") is a chanting, prayer and general-purpose counting application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information you enter and store

Counting progress, selected or custom mantras, chanting history, goals, reminder schedules, language preferences and appearance settings are stored locally on your device to make the app function. The app does not require you to create an account.

Photos and device permissions

If you choose a background image, the app accesses the photo you select and saves a local copy for display in the app. Notification permission may be requested to deliver prayer reminders. The app also uses device vibration and audio playback for optional counting feedback. These features can be controlled through the app or device settings.

Data collection and sharing

JAPAM COUNT does not intentionally upload your chanting records, personal mantras or selected background photos to a developer-operated server. It does not sell your personal information or include advertising functionality. The app's core counting features work without an internet connection.

Third-party services

The app is built using React Native and Expo software libraries and is distributed through Google Play. Device operating systems, Google Play and other platform services may process technical information under their own privacy policies. We do not control their independent processing.

Data retention and deletion

Information saved within JAPAM COUNT remains on your device until you remove it through available app controls, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data may permanently delete locally saved progress.

Children's privacy

JAPAM COUNT is not specifically directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an app-operated account or server.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, permissions or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
